People

Projects

Connected initiatives for sustainable change

DIVD.Charity collaborates with DIVD.Academy and DIVD.Fund to create a stronger ecosystem for community development. Community education centres expand access to learning and vocational training, while our microfinance support helps entrepreneurs with accessible funding, mentorship, and business guidance. An online mentorship platform also connects professionals and learners who are ready to grow.
