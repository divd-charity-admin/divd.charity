Publication requirements

Public transparency obligations for a charitable organisation

A transparent public-benefit organisation explains what it does, how it is governed, and what it publishes for public review. DIVD.Charity maintains this information in a form that is easy to access and easy to understand.

Core obligations

What must be clear

  • Who the organisation is and how it is registered
  • What public benefit activities it supports
  • Who is responsible for governance and decision-making
  • How the public can review the current record
  • Where key documents are published and archived

Published follow-up

How DIVD.Charity responds

  • Public mission and purpose overview on the main website
  • ANBI transparency section for governance and archive access
  • Versioned records available in the archive
  • Direct links to public charity information and participation channels
