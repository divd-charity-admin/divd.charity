About

We build opportunity through practical public benefit.

DIVD.Charity exists to help people grow through access to education, mentorship, community capacity and entrepreneurial opportunity.

Our work focuses on practical support for communities and individuals who want to develop skills, confidence and long-term opportunity.

DIVD ecosystem
